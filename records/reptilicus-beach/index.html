Reptilicus (1961), beach: record

The single-frame example from the June 2026 study. It was made with an earlier single-frame route and is kept because the result holds up. The current route for unbalanced sources is the one used for Counter Attack.

   
Source Reptilicus trailer, from a 35mm trailer reel scan posted publicly on archive.org. 2048 × 1556, 24 fps, 2,941 frames. Sampled frame 25 of 80 (beach, woman). Unbalanced, faded to magenta.
Route Earlier single-frame route: Qwen Image Edit 2509 with a Canny edge control and a blurred colour chart as the reference, then the scan’s own luma composited under the result.
Model qwen_image_edit_2509_fp8_e4m3fn.safetensors
Reference Belak colour patch chart, reduced to 32 px, resized back with bicubic and blurred (Gaussian 18). No people or scene content.
Settings Seed 0, CFG 1.0.
Finished June 2026; shown at SEAPAVAA 2026.
Reproduction No kit. The five stages are published below so the result can be checked.
