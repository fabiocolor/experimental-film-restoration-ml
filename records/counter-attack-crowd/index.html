Counter Attack (1976), crowd shot: record

The whole-shot example on the faded colour recovery page and the home page, and the shot shown on slide 11 of the FIAT/IFTA 2026 talk. It was the first shot finished with what is now the usual route for unbalanced sources: MiniMax H3 with the Fun ControlNet, finished with the Temporal CbCr adapter. The same film’s waterfront shot has its own record.

Source
A faded film copy of Counter Attack (1976), a Chinese feature; the crowd shot with a fast pan in the middle. 385 frames at 1920 × 1440 and 30 frames per second, unbalanced (magenta-red cast). 77 frames were near-duplicates from a broken cadence, so the 308 distinct frames were mapped to 24 frames per second.
Reference
None survives. Two synthetic palettes: the main outdoor palette and one for an indoor close-up, each a Qwen Image Edit 2511 still laid on the source brightness and checked at full size before the run.
Route
Edge map (Canny) of every frame after a small median filter and a local-contrast step → MiniMax H3 with the Fun ControlNet on 124-frame sections at 768 × 576, 24 fps → each H3 frame registered to the source and used as a teacher only if its brightness lined up within 1.5 pixels overall (278 passed) → Temporal CbCr adapter trained on the teachers, best epoch on held-out frames, then fitted on all → colour run at 640 × 480, upscaled with guidance, put on the original brightness at 1920 × 1440 for all 385 frames.
Models
Qwen Image Edit 2511 (palettes); MiniMax H3 with Fun ControlNet Union (original release); the Temporal CbCr adapter (931,274 parameters, trained for this shot).
Finished
30 September 2026. Remaining tint in the shadowed folds of a yellow jacket in the first frames.
On this site
Frames 40 and 230 of the 308-frame comparison video (faded source and result side by side), cut from the video shown at FIAT/IFTA. The comparison video (1920 × 840, 13 s) is on Google Drive.
Reproduced from the published files
Not yet. The H3 and adapter steps are the same as in the balanced-source kit, but this shot's palettes, edge maps and teacher list are not published.
