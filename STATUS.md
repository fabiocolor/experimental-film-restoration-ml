# Project Status

**Updated:** 2026-10-15

The website and download kits for research on machine learning for film restoration, in five areas:

1. Faded Colour Recovery
2. Temporal CbCr (including the CopyCat workflow for Nuke)
3. Diffusion Healing
4. Diffusion Reconstruction
5. Black-and-White Colourization

What works, what does not yet, what can be reproduced and what hardware it takes is on [Where the research stands](https://fabiocolor.github.io/experimental-film-restoration-ml/research-status/). Four [download kits](https://fabiocolor.github.io/experimental-film-restoration-ml/downloads/) reproduce published examples; two of them run in Comfy Cloud.

The examples are workflow demonstrations, not finished restorations. Generated colour or picture is an interpretation unless a known reference supports it.
