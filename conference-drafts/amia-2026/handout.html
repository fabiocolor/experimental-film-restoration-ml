One Local AI Pipeline for
Restoring Faded and Damaged Film

Fabio P. Bedoya Huerta · Independent research in film restoration · info@fabiocolor.com

Whatever survives in the film is evidence.
The models only fill what is lost.

What I am trying to do

Bring colour recovery, temporal colour and picture repair into one local workflow for small archives and nonprofits. Keep the scan, review the shot, and declare generated content. These are workflow demonstrations, not finished restorations.

Why local

Keep scans under archive control. Repeat tests without paying for each cloud run. Record versions and settings so the choices can be checked. Hardware, time and electricity still cost.

Masking versus recovery

Hiding a scratch does not recover the lost photograph. Another surviving frame supplies evidence. A model’s fill is an inference. Record the difference.

What stays from the film, and what the model adds

WorkEvidence keptModel contribution + limit
Faded colourThe scan’s original luma, detail and grain.Inferred chroma. It is not proof of the original colour when no colour reference survives.
Temporal CbCrSource luma and frame geometry. Aligned, reviewed frames teach the adapter.Colour across the shot. Bad alignment or teachers can carry errors into other frames.
Selective healingUsable picture outside the repair mask; surviving frames around a gap.Generated regions or missing frames. Keep a mask or frame list, including blended borders.
Full reconstructionThe surviving elements guide structure and movement.Rebuilt picture when most of the image is unusable. Fine detail can be invented.

Control and review

Hard Canny is the default geometry control. It is separate from colour inference and luma/chroma compositing. Keep source, references, settings, outputs and masks. Review in motion.

Current limits

Flicker, geometry drift, reduced resolution, short sections, slow runs and invented detail. Q47 used ChatGPT Images reference stills, so it is not local from end to end. Higher resolution and fewer manual steps remain open.
