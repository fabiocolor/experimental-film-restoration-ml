# Research Roadmap

## Faded colour recovery

- Get at least three out of four new shots working on the first pass.
- One app that runs the current route on new shots.
- Done in October 2026: the single-frame graph and the balanced palette still as self-contained ComfyUI graphs that run locally or in Comfy Cloud. Next: the H3 and adapter steps as graphs others can run without the Python steps.

## Temporal CbCr

- Publish the Temporal CbCr adapter.
- Make alignment of historical references less manual, especially across cuts and changing speeds.

## Diffusion healing and reconstruction

- More resolution and longer sections than the current 768-pixel, ten-second limit.
- Better faces and lip movement on reconstructed shots.
- Publish the first pass of the healing workflow.

## Black-and-white colourization

- Fix skin on the earliest shots and foliage in some exteriors.
- Publish examples once they're cleared.

## Across the site

- Publish failures and limits alongside results.
- Keep the original scan, the references and the choices that shaped every result on record.
