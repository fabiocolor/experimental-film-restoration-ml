# Project names

Current names and purposes, clarified on 23 October 2026:

- **[Diffusion Full Reconstruction]({{ '/diffusion-full-reconstruction/' | relative_url }})**:
  whole-shot regeneration when all or most surviving picture evidence is
  unusable in its current state. Tinterillo is the example.
- **[Diffusion Healing]({{ '/diffusion-healing/' | relative_url }})**: specific
  defects, missing areas, partial frames and bounded frame gaps when substantial
  usable evidence survives, as in Poison Pen and Knights of the Trail.
- **[Diffusion Upscaling]({{ '/diffusion-upscaling/' | relative_url }})**:
  reference-based video upscaling and basic resolution/detail enhancement.
  This is a new research idea with no qualified workflow or results.

## Reading older names

The Tinterillo project was previously called **Diffusion Upscaling**, then
**Diffusion Reconstruction**. Its current name is **Diffusion Full
Reconstruction**. The older Poison Pen and Knights project was called
**Diffusion Reconstruction** and is now **Diffusion Healing**. Read the subject,
date and owning project together; the old word alone is ambiguous.

The new Upscaling project is separate from the old use of that name. Historical
Tinterillo upscaling experiments do not become results of the new project.
The conference headings retain the title used in the talk and now explain the
current mapping. Previous changelog entries retain their dated terminology.

## Stable records and addresses

Historical job IDs, checksums, workflow files, media paths and submitted decisions
keep their original meaning. The `/upscaling/`, `/spatial-recovery/` and
`/diffusion-reconstruction/` page addresses lead to Full Reconstruction.
`/downloads/upscaling-stairs/` and the older media folders also describe that
work. The new project uses `/diffusion-upscaling/`.

Classify an operation by its purpose and the usable evidence, rather than by
the model name or whether it processes whole frames. One film can need different
operations on different shots, with separate records and review for each.
