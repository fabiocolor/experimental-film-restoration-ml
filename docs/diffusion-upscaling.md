# Diffusion Upscaling

A separate research idea for reference-based video upscaling and, at its most
basic, video resolution/detail enhancement. This scope was defined on 23 October
2026. No model, reference-conditioning method, workflow or result has been
qualified for this new project yet.

The aim is to increase resolution and detail while retaining the video's
content, geometry and movement. A first experiment should declare what the
reference supplies and compare the result against the input and basic scaling,
including checks for invented detail and temporal consistency. These are
proposed evaluation needs, not measured capabilities.

[Diffusion Full Reconstruction]({{ '/diffusion-full-reconstruction/' | relative_url }})
regenerates whole shots when most surviving picture evidence is unusable in its
current state. [Diffusion Healing]({{ '/diffusion-healing/' | relative_url }})
repairs specific losses when substantial usable evidence survives. Upscaling
has its own purpose, even when the same family of models could be involved.

The older Tinterillo work was called diffusion upscaling in the FIAT/IFTA talk.
Those experiments and results belong to Full Reconstruction. The new Upscaling
project inherits none of their qualification or approvals. The old stairs-kit
and media addresses are kept so existing links continue to work. See
[project names]({{ '/project-names/' | relative_url }}).
