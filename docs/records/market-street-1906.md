# A Trip Down Market Street (1906): record

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Source | *A Trip Down Market Street* (1906, Miles Brothers): the camera on the front of a cable car, the street ahead. 155 frames at 1440 × 1080 (shown pillarboxed in 1920 × 1080). |
| Where it came from | Published 1906, US public domain. The film is held by the [Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/item/00694408); the copy used is a public scan. |
| Route | Three Qwen Image Edit 2511 colour frames from the shot itself (source frames 8, 77 and 146), placed on the source picture → MiniMax H3 as a colour donor at 1024 × 768 → the source luminance kept byte-exact, the colour integrated into it rather than pasted on top. Whole frame, no masks. |
| Models | `qwen_image_edit_2511_fp8mixed`; MiniMax H3 ref2va with its text encoder and VAE. |
| Review | Four passes. The first three were approved with notes on the palette (too weak, then not yet of professional grade). The fourth, shown on the site, was approved with notes: the colour integration is better, and the limit now is the softness of the source, which gives the model less to hold on to. |
| Reproduction | Not yet. |

The palette is an interpretation, not historical fact.
