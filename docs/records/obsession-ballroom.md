# Obsession (1976), the ballroom: record

The reveal on the home page and the first whole-shot example on the [faded colour recovery]({{ '/colour-recovery/' | relative_url }}) page.

<dl class="facts">
  <dt>Source</dt><dd><em>Obsession</em> (1976) trailer, from the 35mm trailer reel scan posted publicly on <a href="https://archive.org/details/film-bar-70-trailers-reel-1440x-1080-24fps">archive.org</a>: the couple dancing in the ballroom, with the camera moving. 254 frames, 24 fps, 2048 × 1556, faded to red. No colour reference survives.</dd>
  <dt>Reference</dt><dd>Four anchor frames (32, 96, 184 and 224), one per section, chosen at full size from TeleStyle candidates on the exact frames. One candidate (frame 159) was rejected because it invented a navy colour on the woman's shoulder; frame 184 was chosen over 190 on 11 August 2026.</dd>
  <dt>Route</dt><dd>MiniMax H3 in reference-to-video mode on four overlapping sections of 124 frames at 768 × 576, each section used for the middle of its range; the colour of the result lined up with the source and put on the scan's own brightness at 2048 × 1556.</dd>
  <dt>Models</dt><dd>MiniMax H3 (reference-to-video) with its text encoder and VAE; TeleStyle (a LoRA for Qwen Image Edit 2511) for the anchor candidates.</dd>
  <dt>Finished</dt><dd>The four anchors were chosen at full size before the run; the composite is the result shown here.</dd>
  <dt>On this site</dt><dd>9.5 seconds of the shot, source and result as delivered.</dd>
  <dt>Reproduction</dt><dd>Not yet. The anchors, the section videos and the alignment step are not published; the H3 step is the same as in the <a href="{{ '/downloads/faded-colour-recovery-t185/' | relative_url }}">balanced-source kit</a>.</dd>
</dl>
