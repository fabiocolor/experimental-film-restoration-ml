# Counter Attack (1976), the quarry: record

The reveal in the home page's first area and the third example on the [faded colour recovery]({{ '/colour-recovery/' | relative_url }}) page. The same film's [crowd]({{ '/records/counter-attack-crowd/' | relative_url }}) and [waterfront]({{ '/records/counter-attack-waterfront/' | relative_url }}) shots have their own records.

<dl class="facts">
  <dt>Source</dt><dd>A faded film copy of <em>Counter Attack</em> (反击, 1976): the quarry, a long shot of 681 frames at 1920 × 1440 and 30 frames per second, unbalanced, faded to red. No colour reference survives.</dd>
  <dt>Reference</dt><dd>Synthetic palette stills from Qwen Image Edit 2511, with the palette changing between the second and third sections as the scene changes.</dd>
  <dt>Route</dt><dd>MiniMax H3 on six overlapping sections; each seam chosen in a calm passage and blended over a few frames (a longer blend, 127–194, carries the palette change). The colour put on the scan's own brightness; the assembly itself takes four minutes once the sections exist.</dd>
  <dt>Models</dt><dd>Qwen Image Edit 2511 (palettes); MiniMax H3 (reference-to-video).</dd>
  <dt>Finished</dt><dd>9 August 2026. The H3 result was a clear step up from the earlier Wan route and became the method for the shots that followed.</dd>
  <dt>On this site</dt><dd>9 seconds of the shot. The source panel is cut from a review video (480 × 360); the result is the full-size composite.</dd>
  <dt>Reproduction</dt><dd>Not yet.</dd>
</dl>
